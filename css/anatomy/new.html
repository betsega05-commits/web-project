Traditional Kitfo

Kitfo is a traditional Ethiopian dish of minced raw beef, marinated in mitmita (a chili powder-based spice blend) and niter kibbeh (a clarified butter infused with spices). Served with freshly made injera and ayib (Ethiopian cottage cheese).

Our Kitfo is prepared with the highest quality beef and authentic Ethiopian spices, offering an unforgettable culinary experience.

The Heart of Ethiopian Culture

Hagerigna Restaurant

Welcome to Hagerigna Restaurant, where Ethiopian tradition, culture, and flavor come together. We are dedicated to providing our guests with an authentic Ethiopian dining experience, featuring traditional dishes prepared with rich spices, fresh ingredients, and care. From Doro Wat and Kitfo to Tibs and Injera, our menu celebrates the diverse flavors of Ethiopian cuisine. Every dish is prepared to give our customers the taste and feeling of traditional Ethiopian hospitality. At Hagerigna Restaurant, we believe that food is more than a meal—it is a way to **share culture, create memories, and bring people together**. Come and experience the taste of Ethiopia with us.
